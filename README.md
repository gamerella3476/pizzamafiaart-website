This website's main pages
Index: Recent posts, ie: new pages I've added.
About Me: A page about the artist, and contact info.
Portfolio: A selection of artwork I've made to show to potential hiring mangers, job interviews, and clients.
Blog: status updates on my life, what I've been doing, my thoughts and ideas.
D&D journals: Transcripts of my D&D journals for the campaigns I've played in. Along with scans of the journals, character info, and other misc information about the campaigns.
Major Projects: Large-form projects that I'm proud of. Zines, Games, Major artworks.
Art Archive: a catch-all for my artwork. Scanned sketchbook pages, concept art, character design, anything that doesn't fit into a neat category.
