Louie's Journal

Road Through Varom

Dec 6, 2020 - March 1, 2026 (COMPLETED)
Image

Louie Ricotta, a Sorcerer, and Aasimar, and a person haunted beyond belief.

Follow the the Khedloh-Surefoot expedition into the holdlands, to reclaim lands lost in the Great Extinction nearly 600 years ago. The party must not only travel down the main roads of Varom to gather gold, supplies, and allies. but also traverse through the unpaved trails of land reclaimed by nature and foul beasts. All the while, each party member has their own demons to face... some of them, being REAL demons.
